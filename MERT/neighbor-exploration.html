    What to decide for the listening report

    Opening question. Choose “All kits” to show how strongly kit sound dominates. I recommend opening with “Other kits only”: it gives listeners a new question and yields both successful matches and misses.

    Listening experience. I’d add a reference player and five ranked play buttons, then let readers switch between a recovered groove and a miss. Similarity scores and snare labels beside the players explain what changed.

    Depth. I’d keep the full heatmap expandable. It serves readers who want to inspect relationships across all kit pairs. A separate hat-swap experiment earns its place if you want to explain the cause.
